# Storefront (home, shop, product, cart, saved)

Scope: the whole public storefront. Mode: Persuade on the home page, Operate on shop, product, cart and saved.
Audience and job: phone shoppers from Instagram choose a pair and send the order on WhatsApp. Constraints: PRODUCT.md (no invented ratings or claims, no other brands' marks, card payment not live).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The store is a shoe-shop app in the hand: the catalogue is the interface (grey product tiles with cart and heart controls, search, category chips, a tab bar), refusing the editorial landing page of flyers, glass panels and banners.

OWN-WORLD: White ground; #F3F3F3 tiles; ink #111 for every primary control; greys for secondary text; red only for a saved heart; colour comes only from the shoes. Poppins 400-700, sentence-case headings, bold uppercase only in the hero. 16px card radius, pill chips, round icon buttons, soft offset shadows. Hero photography in black and white.

STORY: See the brand and real pairs, search or tap a category, save or add a pair, pick colour, size and quantity, send the order on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: rounded full-bleed B&W hero, headline bold uppercase white centred near the bottom, one white button with an arrow, slide dots; tab bar fixed below with a raised black Home disc. Desktop: the same hero, wide, text bottom-left. Directly after: store row, search with filter button, Categories chips with arrows, product grid.

FORM: Pinned by the owner's mockup (2026-10-15); concept roll skipped because the brief pins the world. Signature interaction: the product "stand" (ring platform under the photo, black disc turns through photos) plus a springy heart save.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
